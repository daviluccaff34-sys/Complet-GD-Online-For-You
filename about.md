# Complet GD Online For You!

Complet GD Online For You! is an Android-first Geometry Dash Geode mod project.

The long-term goal is to bring friends together through online rooms, collaborative level creation, cloud-backed project storage, and an assistant that explains Geometry Dash triggers.

Features will be introduced in stages. Online services will be connected only after their interfaces, security rules, and failure handling have been designed and reviewed.

**Current status:** initial project scaffold. The planned features are not implemented yet.
